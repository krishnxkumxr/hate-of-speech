🧠 How It Works

Emotion Classification

The emotion classifier analyzes your tweet using keyword-based sentiment analysis. It detects 7 distinct emotions:

😊 Happy: Positive emotions (love, beautiful, amazing, wonderful)
😐 Neutral: Factual content (information, report, question)
😲 Surprised: Unexpected reactions (wow, shocked, amazing)
😠 Angry: Negative emotions (hate, angry, terrible, awful)
😢 Sad: Depressive emotions (sad, lonely, heartbreak)
🤢 Disgusted: Repulsive content (disgusting, gross, nasty)
😨 Fear: Anxiety (fear, scared, panic, worried)

Hate Speech Detection

Uses Logistic Regression classifier trained on 17,197 tweets. The model:
1. Preprocesses text (removes mentions, special characters, stemming)
2. Extracts features using TF-IDF vectorization
3. Computes probability of hate speech (0.0 - 1.0)
4. Classifies based on 0.3 probability threshold

Text Preprocessing Pipeline

Your tweet goes through 6 steps before analysis:

Step 1: Remove @Mentions
Eliminates Twitter handles (@user)
Step 2: Remove Special Chars
Removes symbols, numbers, punctuation
Step 3: Remove Short Words
Filters words with 3 or fewer characters
Step 4: Tokenization
Splits text into individual words
Step 5: Convert to Lowercase
Normalizes all text to lowercase
Step 6: Stemming
Reduces words to root form (ing, ed, ly)
